## My Mantra

- **Platform as a Product** - I believe in building "paved roads" for developers. Infrastructure should be self-serve, intuitive, and secure by default, allowing product teams to ship with high velocity.
- **Blameless Culturer** - I prioritize systemic fixes over human blame. The most reliable systems are built by teams that feel psychologically safe to investigate root causes and implement automated guardrails.
- **Policy over Proces** - Instead of relying on manual PR reviews for infrastructure compliance, I favor policy-as-code via Github Self-hosted Action runner framework and cluster policy enforcement (Kyverno) to automate governance and prevent misconfigurations before they reach production.
- **Data-Driven Engineering** - Every architectural or cost optimization decision is validated through deep observability and progressive rollouts.
